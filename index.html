Interactive demonstration · SQLite-accurate

A library, connected.

A faithful browser adaptation of Mendure DME ↗ — same tables, same rules. Pick a customer (phone + ZIP validated, like the real app), select one or more items, check out, then sign the digital agreement. Loans stay *pending* until signed.

Real schema: customers(id, name, phone, zip_code, date_added) · equipment(equipment_id PK, item_name) · loans(customer_id FK, equipment_id FK, checked_out_date, due_date, returned_date, agreement_pending) · customer_agreements · checkout_log — Equipment IDs match AA-0000; due is checkout + 120 business days (Mon–Fri minus federal holidays).

Loan ledger

LoanEquipment (equipment_id)Customer (phone)Checked outDue (120 business days)AgreementAction

Digital agreement — witness + signature

Mirroring customer_agreements: waiver + digital signature, witness name, agreed date. Until signed, loans keep agreement_pending = 1.

Faithful to src/main/features/{customers,equipment,loans}.js + src/shared/business-logic.js: phone needs 10 digits, ZIP 5 digits, duplicate phones rejected, equipment IDs upper-cased to AA-0000, due rolls past weekends + observed federal holidays, extend recalculates from today, return stamps returned_date. Fictional records only; the real app is offline Electron + SQLite (WAL) with login + reports + Excel/CSV import-export.
