Interactive demonstration

A library, connected.

An interactive browser adaptation of Mendure DME ↗, the real Electron and SQLite project. Choose a fictional customer, then check out equipment to see linked records and the 120-day return date.

The actual schema connects loans.customer_id → customers.id and loans.equipment_id → equipment.equipment_id. Equipment IDs follow AA-0000; a returned item sets returned_date.
